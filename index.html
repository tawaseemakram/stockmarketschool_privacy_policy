6. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliant)

We believe you should have complete control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated cloud records at any time:

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate)

  1. Open the Stock Market School app.
  2. Navigate to the Profile section from the bottom navigation or menu.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile page.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Delete Account (खाता हटाएं / ಖಾತೆ ಅಳಿಸಿ)".
  5. Confirm your choice in the verification dialog.

Upon confirmation, all your stored progress records, quiz scores, certificates, and user identifiers will be permanently deleted from our Firebase Firestore database, and your authentication session will be closed.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you may submit a data deletion request by emailing us at tawaseemakram@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Stock Market School", specifying the Google account email used to sign in. We will process and permanently remove your records within 7 business days.
